# Libera

**Libera** (Libera Earth Radiation Budget Investigation) is a NASA-funded science instrument designed to measure Earth's radiation budget — the balance between the solar energy our planet absorbs and the thermal energy it emits back to space. This balance, known as Earth's energy imbalance, is a critical to understanding our changing planet.

## Overview

Libera will fly aboard NOAA's [JPSS-4](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/our-satellites/currently-flying/joint-polar-satellite-system) (Joint Polar Satellite System-4) satellite and is designed to extend the multi-decadal data record established by NASA's [CERES](https://ceres.larc.nasa.gov/) (Clouds and Earth's Radiant Energy System) instruments. By continuing this unbroken record, Libera will help scientists detect long-term trends in Earth's energy budget.

The instrument is developed by the [Laboratory for Atmospheric and Space Physics (LASP)](https://lasp.colorado.edu) at the University of Colorado Boulder, in partnership with NASA, NOAA, and NIST.

## Key Facts

- **Mission type:** Earth observation — radiation budget monitoring
- **Host satellite:** NOAA JPSS-4
- **Measurements:** Reflected solar radiation and emitted thermal infrared radiation from Earth
- **Mission goal:** Continue the long-term Earth radiation budget data record
- **Partners:** NASA, NOAA, NIST, and LASP/University of Colorado Boulder
